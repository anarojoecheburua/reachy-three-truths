# Three Truths and a Lie: Reachy Mini script (v2, approved by Ana, 17 Oct 2026)

Each block = one audio clip. Reachy only moves to the next block when Ana presses NEXT.

## 01_intro
Hello, Glasgow! I'm Reachy Mini, a small open-source robot made by Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face. Welcome to Science Night! Tonight we're playing Three Truths and a Lie. I'll tell you four things: about me, about robots, or about open source. Three are true. One is a lie. Scan the QR code on the screen and vote for the lie. Then I'll reveal who got it right. Don't worry, I'm a very honest robot. Most of the time.

## 02_round1
Round one is all about me.
A. I'm twenty-eight centimetres tall, and I weigh about one and a half kilos.
B. Nine motors move my head, my body and my two antennas.
C. I arrived fully built, straight out of the box.
D. I listen to you through four microphones.
Which one is the lie? Vote now. I'll wait. Patiently. Like a robot.

## 03_reveal1
The lie was C! I didn't arrive in one piece. I come as a kit. Ana built me herself. She spent an afternoon with a screwdriver to make me. Thank you, Ana. That's really nice of you.

## 04_round2
Round two is about robots.
A. The word "robot" comes from a Czech play written in 1920, from a word meaning forced labour.
B. The first robot to work in a factory was built in Japan, for Toyota.
C. There are more than four million robots working in factories around the world.
D. A robot helicopter has flown on Mars.
Which one is the lie? Vote now!

## 05_reveal2
The lie was B! The first factory robot, called Unimate, started work in 1961 at a General Motors plant in the United States. Japan came later, and then went all in.

## 06_round3
Round three, and the last one, is about open source.
A. Linux, which runs most of the world's web servers, started as a student's hobby project in 1991.
B. Anyone can download my software and my simulator for free.
C. Hugging Face named itself after an emoji.
D. Open source means nobody is allowed to sell it.
Which one is the lie? Vote now!

## 07_reveal3
The lie was D! Open source means anyone can see, change and share the code. You can still sell it. I'm open source, and I'm still for sale. Very reasonable price.

## 08_goodbye
Thank you, Glasgow! You're very good at catching lies. Enjoy the rest of Science Night. Good night!
(then: go to sleep)
