Three Truths and a Lie

A fully scripted party game for Reachy Mini. No LLM, no improvising, works offline.

What it does

Reachy tells the room four statements: about itself, about robots and about open source. Three are true, one is a lie, and the audience votes for the lie. Then Reachy reveals the answer. Built for a live audience at Science Night Glasgow, October 2026.

How it works

1

Open the remote

Start the app, then open the robot's address on port 8042 on your phone.

2

Press NEXT

Reachy wakes up and plays the intro through its own speaker, moving its head as it talks.

3

Say your line, press again

Each press plays the next of 8 pre-recorded clips. Nothing moves on without you.

4

Goodbye

After the last clip Reachy goes back to sleep. STOP / SLEEP works at any time.

Why no AI on the night?

It's a live show, so Reachy must never go off-script. Every line was written in advance, voiced once with Qwen3-TTS (speaker "Aiden"), checked by a person and saved as a WAV file. On the robot there's no LLM, no speech generation, no microphone and no internet: it just plays the clips in order.
